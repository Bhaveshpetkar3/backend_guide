<section class="features-section">
  <h2>What Makes This Different</h2>
  <p class="section-subtitle">Built on the R.R.B.M.W. loop — the engine that separates learning from doing.</p>

  <div class="features-grid">
    <div class="feature-card">
      <span class="card-icon">📖</span>
      <h3>Read Primary Sources</h3>
      <p>Official docs, source code, papers. Not blog summaries. You learn the real thing, not folklore.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card">
      <span class="card-icon">🔨</span>
      <h3>Rebuild From Scratch</h3>
      <p>Implement crude versions with no libraries. Transform vocabulary into genuine understanding.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card">
      <span class="card-icon">💥</span>
      <h3>Break It Deliberately</h3>
      <p>Overload it, kill it mid-write, exhaust its memory. Know the failure modes, not just the happy path.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card">
      <span class="card-icon">📊</span>
      <h3>Measure Everything</h3>
      <p>Benchmark it. Get a number. Change one variable. Get another number. Explain the delta.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card">
      <span class="card-icon">✍️</span>
      <h3>Write It Down</h3>
      <p>300–800 words explaining it to a competent engineer. Writing is where fuzzy understanding gets exposed.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card">
      <span class="card-icon">🎯</span>
      <h3>Gates, Not Dates</h3>
      <p>Advance only when you can demonstrate capability without notes. No rushing, no faking.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
